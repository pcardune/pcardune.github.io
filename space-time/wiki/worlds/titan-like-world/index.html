Wiki / Worlds

Titan-like world

A cold world with a thick, hazy atmosphere of nitrogen and methane.

Titan is Saturn's largest moon, and it is the only moon in the solar system with a thick atmosphere. The air is mostly nitrogen, as on Earth, with a few percent methane, and the surface pressure is about 1.5 times Earth's. An orange haze of organic molecules hides the surface from view.

It is also fantastically cold, around -180 °C. At that temperature methane behaves the way water does on Earth. It falls as rain, flows in rivers and gathers in lakes and seas, and water ice acts as the bedrock. The Huygens probe landed there in 2005 and saw pebbles of ice on a plain that had been carved by liquid methane.

Titan is small, but it holds its air because the cold keeps the gas molecules slow (see Atmospheric escape). Sunlight breaks methane apart and makes the haze, and over time the methane would be used up, so something must resupply it.

A Titan-like world is any cold world far from its star with a nitrogen and methane atmosphere. NASA plans to send a rotorcraft, Dragonfly, to fly across Titan's surface.

In Space Time, far from the star, cold worlds can have a nitrogen and methane haze like Titan's.
