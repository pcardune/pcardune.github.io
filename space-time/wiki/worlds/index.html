Worlds

The planets and moons you find, from airless rock to oceans and gas giants.

  • Captured asteroidsSmall, dark, carbon-rich moons that may once have been asteroids.
  • Dwarf planetA small, round world that has not cleared its path, like Pluto and Ceres.
  • Early EarthWhat Earth was like before it had oxygen, and why that matters for other worlds.
  • Gas giantA huge planet of hydrogen and helium with no surface to stand on.
  • Ice giantA smaller, colder giant like Uranus and Neptune, built mostly of water, ammonia and methane.
  • Icy moonsMoons of ice with hidden oceans, among the best places to look for life beyond Earth.
  • Mars-like worldA small, cold planet with thin air, which once may have been wetter.
  • MoonsWorlds that orbit planets, and the five kinds found in the game.
  • Ocean worldA planet covered by one deep, global ocean with no dry land.
  • Planetary ringsThin discs of ice and rock circling a planet, like Saturn's.
  • Rocky planetA world with a solid surface of rock and metal, like Earth, Mars and Venus.
  • Snowball worldA planet frozen over from pole to pole, and how ice keeps it that way.
  • Steam worldA planet so hot that its oceans have boiled into the air.
  • Titan-like worldA cold world with a thick, hazy atmosphere of nitrogen and methane.
  • Venus-like worldA planet wrapped in thick carbon dioxide, with a surface hot enough to melt lead.
  • Volcanic moonsMoons kept molten by the squeeze of their giant planet, like Jupiter's Io.
