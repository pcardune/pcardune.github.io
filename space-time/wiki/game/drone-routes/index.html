Drone routes

A standing delivery from one world to another, run by drones on their own.

A route is a standing order for drones. It names one kind of cargo, a source world to take it from and a target world to deliver it to, both in the same star system. The drones assigned to it loop between the two: load at the source, fly out, unload at the target and fly back, for as long as they last.

Routes run on the galaxy clock. That means a route keeps delivering while the ship is somewhere else or sleeping through the years (see Galaxy time and Cryosleep).

A route can have a rule. For example, drones can deliver only while a reading on the target, such as a gas, is below a chosen level. If the rule isn't met, the drones wait at the source until it is.

A route is running while drones haul, paused while its rule holds them back, out of supply when the source has none of the cargo left, or without drones when all have broken down.

If the player calls off a route, its drones fly to the nearest world and wait there for the ship to collect them.
