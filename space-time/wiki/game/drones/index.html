Drones

Small automatic haulers that carry cargo between worlds while the ship is away.

A drone is a small, uncrewed hauler. It carries much less than the ship's hold and flies slower, but it can work on its own. Once a drone is given a job, it keeps doing it, round and round, without the ship there.

That is the point. Changing a whole planet takes far more cargo than the ship can carry by hand. Drones do the repetitive work while the player does something else, or sleeps (see Cryosleep). They run on the galaxy clock, so a drone keeps hauling however long the ship is away (see Galaxy time).

Drones aren't permanent. Each one can make a limited number of trips, and then it breaks down. Replacements come from a fabricator, a factory in orbit that builds them out of metal (see Fabricator). The service lives vary a little, so a fleet built together doesn't all fail on the same day.

Drones travel with the ship from star to star. In a system, the ship programs them to a route, and they fly out to start work (see Drone routes). At the moment they carry gases and water only.

Real robotic cargo ships already exist. Uncrewed freighters regularly supply the International Space Station, though not on a loop without a pilot.
