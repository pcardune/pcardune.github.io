Hydrogen fuel

What the ship burns to thrust, and where it fills up.

The ship's engine burns hydrogen. Hydrogen is the real thing too. It is what rockets like the Space Shuttle's main engines burned, and it releases a great deal of energy per kilogram (see Hydrogen). The game assumes the ship can drive an engine this way for a long time.

Fuel is limited. The tank holds a good stretch of flight but not an unending one, and thrusting uses it up. If it runs dry, the main engine won't fire. The ship can still turn and brake, so it isn't stranded in the dark, but it can't build speed.

Hydrogen is easy to find in the right places. Gas giants are mostly hydrogen, so a ship can refuel by taking some from a giant while in orbit (see Gas giant and Orbit). The idea is a standard one in space fiction, and some real proposals involve scooping gas from a giant planet's upper atmosphere.

The ship's log gives a warning when the tank is getting low, and another when it is empty, naming the nearest giant that is known.

Fuel is separate from the cargo hold. Hydrogen that you take goes into the tank, not the hold.
