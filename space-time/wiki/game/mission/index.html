Mission

The ship's goal, a short ladder of steps from a candidate world to a living one.

The ship has a mission: to bring life to a world. It is a short ladder of five goals, from finding a place to try to seeing life take hold.

  1. Find a candidate world. Survey planets, especially those in a star's habitable zone, until one rates well enough to work on (see Survey and Habitability score).
  2. Start a drone route. Set drones hauling gas or water to a world (see Drone routes).
  3. Ready a world for microbes. Bring its climate into the range that microbes can live in.
  4. Seed a world. Plant a culture from the Seed Bank on a ready world (see Seed Bank).
  5. Bring a world to life. Keep the colony alive until it has spread.

The goals are checked from what has actually happened in the game, not from any one action, so they can be reached in any order. The ladder is just the order the game expects. Each is recorded once, with the time it was reached, and it stays reached whatever happens to the world later.

Reaching the last goal gives a Mission accomplished screen. The game doesn't end there. Play carries on, and a world can keep changing.

The mission doesn't need a specific story, only a purpose: the ship drifts through the galaxy as a seeder, spreading life.
