A calm space exploration game

Space Time

Fly a lone seeding ship through an infinite galaxy. Find the worlds that could hold life, reshape them, and give life a chance to take over.

Discover. Survey. Terraform. Seed.

You are the pilot of a seeding ship, drifting through a galaxy that never ends. Nature is the only antagonist: there are no enemies and no combat, only a universe that is slow, strange and beautiful.

  1. 1

    Explore

    Launch solar probes and bend their paths with starlight to uncover unmapped star systems.

  2. 2

    Survey

    Orbit planets and moons to measure their mass, atmosphere, water and climate.

  3. 3

    Terraform

    Haul gases and water by hand, then watch the climate respond over centuries.

  4. 4

    Seed life

    Microbes, then plants, then animals. Given time, life carries itself further than you can.

Space Time is in active development. Exploration, surveying, climate simulation, cryosleep and hand-hauled terraforming are playable today. Seeding life, automation drones and crises are on the roadmap.

What makes it tick

An infinite galaxy

Space is a grid of named quadrants, each seeded from its coordinates. The same quadrant always holds the same stars, and the galaxy extends forever in every direction.

Probes and gravity

You only get a handful of probes. Line up shots that curve through the gravity of nearby stars so that one probe discovers several systems.

Real-ish worlds

Spectral classes, habitable zones, frost lines, tidal locking, magnetic fields and escape velocity all shape what each planet turns out to be.

Living climates

Greenhouse gases, ice-albedo feedback, oceans and atmospheric escape interact. Mars slowly loses its air; Venus smothers in CO₂. Your changes ripple through.

Cryosleep

Park in orbit and sleep to speed galaxy time up to a thousandfold. Wake when something worth seeing happens, or when a climate alert goes off.

Everything from orbit

You never land. You work from a ship’s cargo hold with a hydrogen fuel tank that keeps you honest about how far you can go.

How to play

The game teaches you as you go with hints in the ship’s log, but here is the whole opening in five steps.

  1. Fly the ship

    Press ← and → to rotate and ↑ to thrust. The ship always travels the way it faces, builds speed while you thrust, and bleeds it off when you let go. ↓ brakes. Zoom with Z and X.

  2. Launch a solar probe

    Stars start out undiscovered. Press T to target a probe: your heading sets its direction, ↑/↓ set its speed, and a curved line previews the path through nearby gravity. Press Space to fire. You begin with 10 probes, and each planet you survey restocks one, so look for shots that pass through several stars.

  3. Fly into a discovered system

    A probed star reveals its planets, spectral class and habitable zone (the mint band). Fly in. The ship shrinks as you approach so that a solar system feels huge. Click a star to read its info panel.

  4. Orbit and survey

    Get close to a planet and press O to enter orbit. Stay there for a few seconds to survey it. Surveyed planets show their atmosphere, climate and supplies. Press O again to leave, and refuel at gas giants, whose hydrogen keeps the engine running.

  5. Reshape a world

    From orbit, harvest gases and water into the cargo hold, then release them into a rocky world or moon. Open the climate dashboard with D to watch the forecast change. Press S to cryosleep and fast-forward galaxy time, and [ / ] to pick the rate.

Tips

  • Heavier stars bend probe paths harder. Use that to curve a shot through a second or third system.
  • Planets inside the habitable zone are highlighted in mint, and they are the best terraforming candidates.
  • Watch your hydrogen. The log warns at a quarter tank and names the nearest known gas giant.
  • Hover any HUD readout for its exact numbers.
